# Decision 002: OpenWaterUnion Replaces Open Water X, WooCommerce Evaluated Against Shopify

**Decision date:** 2026-10-05
**Status:** Active. Platform choice pending PoC evidence.

## Decision

1. **Brand:** OpenWaterUnion.com replaces Open Water X as the merchandise brand. Pricing, costs, and KPIs in [[open-water-x/Open_Water_X_Merchandise_Strategy]] carry over.
2. **Platform:** Build a WooCommerce proof of concept and score it against Shopify. The final platform is not locked until the go/no-go gates in [[open-water-x/OpenWaterUnion-WooCommerce-PoC-Plan]] are met.

## Rationale

* The vault spec ([[open-water-x/portlandgear-shopify-theme-spec]]) assumed Shopify. Switching without evidence repeats the cost of revisiting later.
* At about $73 contribution per order and 182 orders per year, platform cost and maintenance load matter.
* Bryan chose to test, not assume, so the PoC includes a weighted scorecard.

## Alternatives considered

* **Stay on Shopify without testing:** lowest risk, no evidence on cost or ownership.
* **Commit to WooCommerce now:** not chosen. Maintenance load on a one person operation is unproven.

## Open items

* Trademark and name conflict check for "Open Water Union" (week 0)
* Vault references to "Open Water X" to be updated once the name is cleared

## Revisit

End of PoC week 4, or earlier if the technical gate fails.
