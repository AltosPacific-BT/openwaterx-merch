# Open Water X Merchandise Strategy and Financial Assumptions

## Objectives

Build a premium merchandise brand that complements Pacific Open Water
Swim Co. rather than competing on price.

The merchandise should:

-   Extend the relationship after a swim.
-   Reinforce accomplishment and identity.
-   Generate meaningful profit with relatively low order volume.
-   Maintain premium pricing rather than relying on routine discounts.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Brand Positioning

## Pacific Open Water Swim Co.

Core company collection.

Pricing:

-   Hoodie (Independent Trading 10 oz): **\$79**
-   Tee: **\$28**
-   Silicone cap: **\$20**

Purpose:

-   Timeless company branding.
-   Entry price point without being presented as a budget collection.

## Destination Collections

Examples:

-   Swim Alcatraz
-   Swim Tahoe
-   Alcatraz Swim Club
-   Marathon Swim Club

Pricing:

-   Hoodie: **\$89**
-   Tee: **\$35**
-   Silicone cap: **\$25**

Purpose:

-   Achievement merchandise.
-   Destination specific.
-   More emotional value.
-   Stronger storytelling.

## Archive / Legacy

Purpose:

-   Previous logos.
-   Retired designs.
-   Overstock.
-   Odd sizes.
-   Average quality blanks.

These products may be discounted without affecting the value of the
premium collections.

# Shipping Strategy

-   Free shipping on orders over **\$100**
-   Contiguous United States only.

Purpose:

-   Increase average order value.
-   Encourage customers to add a cap or second item.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Swimmer Reward

Instead of discounting merchandise:

-   One time **\$15 Merchandise Gift Certificate**
-   Valid on purchases of **\$75 or more**
-   One use per swimmer
-   Distributed after a completed swim
-   Valid for approximately one year
-   Stackable with free shipping over \$100

Messaging:

"As a thank you for swimming with Pacific Open Water Swim Co., enjoy a
\$15 merchandise gift certificate toward your first Open Water X
purchase."

This is positioned as a thank-you rather than a sale.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Discount Philosophy

Avoid:

-   Sitewide sales
-   Routine percentage discounts
-   Constant coupon campaigns

Instead:

-   Maintain premium pricing.
-   Use Archive/Legacy for clearance.
-   Use the swimmer gift certificate as the primary customer reward.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Expected Customer Behavior

The typical customer has already invested significant time and money
into training and completing an open water swim.

The purchase is expected to be commemorative rather than purely
functional.

Expected order mix:

  Order Type    Typical Purchase
  ------------- --------------------
  Single Item   Hoodie or tee
  Standard      Hoodie + cap
  Premium       Hoodie + tee + cap
  Gift          Multiple garments

Estimated Average Order Value:

**\$110--120**

# Financial Projection

Assumptions:

-   3.5 orders per week
-   182 orders annually
-   Average order value: \$110
-   Average product cost: \$26

-   Packaging: \~\$2/order

\$20,020

Estimated Contribution:

Approximately **\$13,200 per year** before overhead, design, software

**\$73**

# Long Term Vision

The merchandise business should become a profitable extension of Pacific
Open Water Swim Co.

The objective is not to maximize unit sales through discounts.

The objective is to build a respected premium brand where merchandise
represents participation, achievement, and identity.

Every pricing decision should reinforce that positioning.

The company sells memorable experiences first.

The merchandise allows customers to continue carrying those experiences
long after the swim is over.
